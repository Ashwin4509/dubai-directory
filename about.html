The directory

About This Directory

Dubai Real Estate Directory is an independent guide to RERA-licensed real estate agencies operating in Dubai. It is not affiliated with any agency listed and accepts no payment for placement in these profiles.

What this directory is

Dubai Real Estate Directory is a reference resource for buyers, sellers, tenants and investors researching real estate agencies in Dubai. Each profile covers the agency's specialisation, areas covered, Google rating, and a set of frequently asked questions to help readers understand the type of work the agency does.

The directory covers established agencies that are RERA-registered and active in the Dubai market. Agency profiles are compiled from publicly available information: the agency's own website, Google Maps listings, and the Dubai Land Department's licensed broker register.

This directory does not rank agencies or recommend one over another. Its purpose is to present consistent, factual profile information across a set of agencies so that readers can make their own informed comparisons.

How ratings are sourced

Google ratings shown on each agency profile are sourced from Google Maps and reflect the agency's public Google Business Profile rating at the time of the last check. Ratings and review counts are checked periodically and the check date is shown on each profile.

Google ratings are aggregated from reviews left by past clients on Google. They are shown here as a data point — one signal among several — and should not be the sole basis for selecting an agency. An agency with fewer reviews may still be an excellent choice for your specific needs.

Ratings were last checked in July 2026. If you notice a rating that appears significantly out of date, email us and we will re-check it.

Independence and editorial policy

Dubai Real Estate Directory is independent. It is not owned by, funded by, or affiliated with any real estate agency, property developer, or brokerage listed in the directory. Agency profiles are not paid placements — every agency listed is included on the same editorial basis.

The directory offers a Featured Placement option, which allows agencies to have their profile highlighted on the homepage listing. Featured status is clearly labelled. Standard profiles are not affected by whether an agency has a Featured Placement — the profile content remains identical in either case.

We do not accept payment to write favourable profile content, inflate ratings, or remove information from profiles. All profile information reflects publicly available data at the time of writing.

How to use this directory

Filter by specialisation

Use the filters on the homepage to find agencies by type: off-plan, residential, luxury, property management, or international buyer advisory.

Read the full profile

Each agency profile covers services offered, areas covered, key stats, and FAQs — enough to understand whether an agency fits your needs before contacting them.

Verify before engaging

Always confirm the agency's current RERA registration through the DLD broker register before signing any agreement. The link is on every profile.

Verifying an agency's registration

All real estate brokers and agencies in Dubai are required to hold a valid RERA licence issued by the Dubai Land Department. Before engaging any agency, verify that their registration is current by searching the DLD licensed broker register.

Profiles in this directory link directly to the DLD register so you can verify registration in a single click. This directory is not a substitute for that verification — always confirm directly with the DLD that the agency you are working with holds a valid licence.
