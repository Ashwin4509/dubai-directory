Area overview

JVC (Jumeirah Village Circle) is a master-planned residential community developed by Nakheel, located in the heart of new Dubai between Hessa Street and Al Khail Road. It houses over 300 apartment and villa sub-communities spread across a circular layout divided into districts. JVC is not a single building or tower cluster — it is a large community where values, yields and building quality vary significantly, making agency guidance particularly important.

JVC's appeal is straightforward: it offers the largest number of affordable apartments in Dubai closest to the city's main employment hubs (Media City, JLT, Dubai Marina, DIFC) at AED 900–1,400/sqft. For investors, it regularly posts the highest transaction count of any single area in Dubai, meaning both entry and exit are liquid. For tenants, it is one of the most searched areas for mid-range apartments.

The community has evolved significantly since early development. Buildings completed post-2020 typically offer better specifications, fit-out quality and amenities. Investors should focus on newer inventory from established developers.

JVC is a freehold area — any nationality can buy with full ownership rights. No UAE residency is required.

Prices & yields (2026)

JVC offers the highest gross yields of any well-established Dubai community. One-bedroom apartments in good buildings achieve AED 55,000–80,000/year in rent on purchase prices of AED 700,000–950,000, producing gross yields of 7–9%. Two-bedrooms follow a similar yield profile. Short-let is possible but JVC is primarily a long-let market — tenants here are typically professionals working nearby rather than tourists.

Notable buildings

The buildings below are among the most recognisable and liquid JVC inventory. Quality varies widely across JVC, so always check the specific building and developer.

  • Bloom Towers (A, B, C) — JVC's landmark cluster. Three 25+ floor towers with large unit sizes, rooftop pool, gym. Popular with families and professionals. AED 1,100–1,300/sqft.
  • Binghatti Orchid / Binghatti Crest / Binghatti Corner — Binghatti's JVC projects are known for their distinctive architectural style and better finishing. Strong resale demand. AED 1,000–1,350/sqft.
  • Seasons Community — Established mid-rise complex known for affordable entry, consistent tenant demand and proximity to Circle Mall. AED 900–1,050/sqft.
  • Diamond Views — Long-standing JVC project, entry-level pricing, high volume. Good for investors prioritising yield over building spec.
  • Belgravia (I, II, III) — Ellington Properties' JVC projects. Premium finishing and amenities for JVC, strong appreciation track record. AED 1,200–1,400/sqft.
  • One Park Avenue — Reportage Properties project with modern design. Competitive pricing, good rental demand from nearby workers.

Buying process in JVC

Buying in JVC follows the standard Dubai freehold process. Key steps:

  1. Choose and offer. Once you identify a unit, submit an offer in writing. The seller (or seller's agent) counters. When agreed, a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU / Form F) is signed and you pay a 10% deposit to hold the property.
  2. No Objection Certificate (NOC). For resale units, the seller must get an NOC from the developer (Nakheel for original plots; varies by sub-developer) confirming no outstanding service charges. This typically takes 5–10 business days.
  3. DLD transfer. The title transfer happens at a Dubai Land Department Trustee Office. You pay the 4% DLD Transfer Fee plus AED 4,200 admin fee. The buyer's agent fee is typically 2% of the purchase price. Mortgage buyers need their lender present.
  4. Title Deed. DLD issues the Title Deed in your name, which is the definitive proof of ownership.

Always verify your broker's RERA registration before signing anything. Check their BRN directly at dubailand.gov.ae.

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy property in JVC?
Yes. Jumeirah Village Circle is a designated freehold area under UAE law. Any nationality can purchase property with full freehold ownership rights, and no UAE residency is required. Your title deed is issued by the Dubai Land Department in your name.
What is the average price per sq ft in JVC?
As of 2026, resale apartments in JVC typically range from AED 900 to AED 1,400 per sq ft depending on building age, developer, floor and view. Post-2020 buildings from established developers sit at the upper end, while older mid-tier stock sits at the lower end. Off-plan launches start from around AED 950 per sq ft.
What rental yields does JVC offer?
Gross rental yields in JVC typically range from 7% to 9% annually, making it one of Dubai's highest-yielding established communities. One-bedroom apartments in good buildings rent for AED 55,000–80,000 per year on purchase prices of AED 700,000–950,000. JVC is primarily a long-let market driven by professionals working nearby.
Is JVC good for first-time buyers?
Yes. JVC offers affordable entry prices, the highest transaction volume of any single Dubai area (so buying and selling are liquid) and consistently strong rental demand from professionals working in Media City, JLT, Dubai Marina and DIFC. Because building quality varies, first-time buyers should favour newer inventory from established developers and use an agency with JVC-specific experience.
Which agencies are most active in JVC?
McCone Properties, haus & haus, Provident Estate and Harbor Real Estate all cover JVC actively, across resale, leasing, off-plan and property management. All are listed on Dubai Real Estate Directory.
