🔍 2. Google Search Console
Goal: Find keywords that are getting impressions but low clicks (position 5–20). These are your quick wins — the site is nearly ranking, and one improvement can push it to page 1. Also watch for any manual penalties or crawl errors.
  • GSCCheck Performance → Last 28 days
    Open GSC → Performance. Click Queries tab. Sort by Impressions. Look for queries with 100+ impressions but CTR under 5% — these are your improvement targets. Write the top 3 here or note them.
  • GSCCheck Pages tab — what's ranking?
    Click Pages tab. Which pages get the most clicks? Which pages get impressions but zero clicks? Zero-click pages often have weak title tags or meta descriptions — improving them can unlock traffic without writing new content.
  • GSCCheck Indexing → Coverage
    Go to Indexing → Pages. Check the "Not indexed" count. If new pages (agency profiles, articles) are there, click in to see why. Common reasons: "Discovered — currently not indexed" (Google hasn't crawled yet — wait) or "Crawled — currently not indexed" (quality issue — investigate).

    From the Queries list, choose one keyword ranking positions 8–20 with decent impressions. Open the page it lands on. Ask: is this keyword in the H1? Is it in the meta title? Is there enough content? Make one improvement. Small, consistent changes compound over weeks.

📊 3. GA4 — User Behaviour
Goal: Understand what real users do on the site. GSC shows how people find you. GA4 shows what they do after they arrive. Together they give the full picture.
  • GA4Check Sessions and Users — last 7 days
    Open GA4 → Reports → Overview. Note total sessions and users for the week. Is it growing week-on-week? If it dropped: was there a GSC indexing issue? Did you publish less that week? Track the trend in a simple note — even just "Week of Oct 18: 120 users" is useful data over time.
  • GA4Check top pages — what are people reading?
    Go to Reports → Engagement → Pages and screens. Which pages get the most views? Which agency profiles are most visited? This tells you what users care about — if AX Capital or Allsopp always top the list, users trust those brands. If a newer agency profile is rising, that's a signal to expand its content.
  • GA4Check Engagement Rate
    Engagement Rate (in GA4) = sessions where the user spent 10+ seconds or visited 2+ pages. A rate above 50% is healthy. Below 40% means people are bouncing — often because the page didn't answer their question. If a specific page has low engagement, it may need clearer content or better internal links.
  • GA4Note one insight to act on
    Based on what you saw: write one specific action. Examples: "JVC article gets views but high bounce — add internal links to Harbor Real Estate profile." Or: "Luxury article top page — publish follow-up on Palm Jumeirah agencies." Small, weekly improvements add up fast.
