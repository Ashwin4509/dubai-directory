Our editorial policy

This directory is independent. Featured placement is a visibility upgrade — it does not change the factual content of your profile. We do not write favourable reviews, inflate ratings, or remove accurate information in exchange for payment.

Buyers trust this directory because they know featured agencies paid for position, not for a false endorsement. We mark featured agencies clearly. This transparency is what makes featured placement worth having.

Frequently asked questions

Does being featured change what my profile says?
No. The profile content — services, areas covered, Google rating, FAQs — is the same for all agencies. Featured placement changes your position in the directory and adds a visual distinction (badge, logo, enquiry button). It does not change the editorial content of your profile.
How many featured spots are available?
We cap featured placements to ensure genuine visibility. There are currently 5 featured spots available on the homepage. Once filled, we maintain a waiting list.
What kind of buyers use this directory?
Buyers, investors and expats who are actively researching agencies before making contact. They come via Google searches for agency names, comparison queries ("best real estate agency Dubai"), and expat research. These are high-intent visitors — people actively looking for an agency, not passive browsers.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
No contract and no minimum term. Featured placement is month-to-month. Cancel any time with 7 days notice before your next billing date.
My agency isn't listed yet. Can I still get featured?
Yes. Submit your details via the form and we will create your profile and apply featured placement at the same time. We cover RERA-licensed agencies operating in Dubai.
