About Unique Properties

Founded in 2008, Unique Properties has operated through the full spectrum of Dubai's off-plan market cycles — from the project-rich pre-2008 environment through the correction that followed, the recovery period through 2013–2019 and the sharp upturn of the post-2021 cycle. This experience across multiple developer generations gives the agency's agents a grounded view of which developers and project types have performed well, and which have delivered below their marketed specifications.

The agency's primary focus is on investment-grade off-plan property in Dubai's central and waterfront corridors — Business Bay, Downtown Dubai, Creek Harbour and Dubai Hills Estate. These areas attract both local and international investor demand, and Unique Properties' multilingual team and international buyer infrastructure (POA services, remote advisory, post-handover management referrals) make them accessible to clients purchasing Dubai property from outside the UAE.

With 328 Google reviews and a 4.7 rating, Unique Properties has a solid client satisfaction record across a meaningful volume of transactions. The agency covers both primary developer launches and the secondary market, which means clients who purchased off-plan through the agency can later re-engage them to manage resale or leasing when their property is handed over — avoiding the need to establish a new brokerage relationship after handover.

Frequently asked questions

What is Unique Properties' strength in the Dubai market?

Unique Properties has been operating since 2008 with a focus on off-plan sales and international buyers. Their longevity in the off-plan space means agents have guided clients through multiple launch cycles and have first-hand knowledge of which developers have delivered on time and to specification. This institutional knowledge is particularly valuable for buyers wanting to avoid the lower-quality end of the off-plan market.

What is Business Bay like as an investment area?

Business Bay is a mixed-use district adjacent to Downtown Dubai on the Dubai Canal. Rental yields are typically solid at 5–7%, driven by demand from professionals working in Downtown and DIFC who prefer Business Bay's lower rents versus Downtown itself. Canal-facing buildings command a notable premium. Unique Properties has depth in Business Bay and can advise on which buildings and unit types offer the best investment fundamentals.

Does Unique Properties work with buyers outside the UAE?

Yes. International buyers are a core client segment for Unique Properties. The agency has multilingual capabilities and experience guiding non-resident buyers through the full Dubai purchase process — including remote advisory, POA arrangements for DLD registration, mortgage options for non-residents and post-handover property management referrals.

What is Creek Harbour and why is it growing?

Dubai Creek Harbour is a large-scale Emaar master development around the restored Dubai Creek waterway, east of Downtown. It offers waterfront-adjacent units at a discount to Palm Jumeirah while delivering a similar water-view lifestyle. Strong off-plan demand has made it one of Dubai's most active launch corridors. Unique Properties covers Creek Harbour and can advise on current launch activity and pricing.
