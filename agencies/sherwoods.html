About Sherwoods

Founded in 2002, Sherwoods entered the Dubai market in the early years of freehold property ownership for foreigners, when the city's real estate sector was still taking shape. Two decades of operating through multiple market cycles — the 2008–2009 correction, the 2014–2016 softening, and the post-2020 recovery — has given the agency a longitudinal understanding of Dubai property that newer agencies cannot replicate.

As an independent brokerage, Sherwoods does not carry the institutional structure or branch network of the large franchise agencies. What it offers instead is depth of experience and continuity of team in specific markets. The agency is particularly active in the secondary market — completed, ready properties — where knowledge of specific communities, building conditions and fair pricing matters most.

Property management is a meaningful part of the business. Sherwoods manages rental properties on behalf of overseas investors, handling tenant finding, tenancy contracts, rent collection and maintenance oversight. For investors buying Dubai property without intending to live there, this full-cycle capability — from sourcing the property through to managing it post-purchase — is a genuine differentiator.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sherwoods a reputable agency in Dubai?
Sherwoods has been operating in Dubai since 2002 — one of the longest-established independent agencies in the city. That longevity is itself a signal of consistent performance; agencies that do not deliver reliably do not survive two decades in Dubai's competitive market. As with any agency, check the specific agent's track record for your target community before committing.
What types of properties does Sherwoods specialise in?
Sherwoods covers residential sales and lettings across Dubai's established communities, with particular strength in the secondary (completed property) market. They are known for villa and townhouse community transactions as well as apartment sales and lettings in mid-market to premium segments. Their longevity means they have sold in most of Dubai's established freehold areas.
Does Sherwoods offer property management?
Yes. Property management — overseeing a rental property on behalf of an owner, including tenant sourcing, rent collection and maintenance coordination — is a core part of Sherwoods' offer. This makes them a useful choice for overseas investors who want Dubai property exposure without managing the day-to-day themselves.
