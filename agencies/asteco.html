About Asteco

Founded in 1985, Asteco predates Dubai's modern property boom by nearly two decades. The firm was established during the early years of the UAE's real estate market and has grown alongside it, building a depth of market knowledge that is difficult to replicate. With over 40 years in the UAE market, Asteco's experience spans multiple property cycles — boom years, corrections and recoveries — giving them a longitudinal perspective that newer agencies simply cannot offer.

Asteco's business is broader than most agencies in this directory. While many brokerages focus on sales and leasing, Asteco has a significant property management arm — managing large-scale residential and commercial buildings for institutional owners and developers. They also run a professional valuations practice, staffed by RICS-qualified surveyors, which serves mortgage lenders, investors and developers who need formal assessments for financial or legal purposes.

For individual buyers and tenants, Asteco offers residential sales and leasing across Dubai's established and emerging communities. For landlords and investors with portfolios, Asteco's property management track record is a core consideration. Their longevity in the UAE market has allowed them to build relationships across the developer and institutional landscape that are unavailable to newer entrants.

Frequently asked questions

What is Asteco best known for in Dubai?
Asteco is best known as one of the UAE's oldest property companies and for their depth in property management. They manage large portfolios of residential and commercial buildings for institutional owners, alongside residential sales, leasing and professional valuations. Their longevity in the UAE gives them experience across multiple property cycles that newer agencies lack.
Does Asteco handle residential property for individual buyers?
Yes. Asteco offers residential sales and leasing services for individual buyers and tenants in addition to their institutional property management and commercial agency work. Their coverage spans Dubai and other UAE emirates.
Does Asteco provide property valuations?
Yes. Asteco's valuations practice provides formal assessments for mortgage purposes, portfolio reporting, dispute resolution and insurance. They are staffed by RICS-qualified surveyors — a professional qualification that is relevant when valuations need to meet formal lender or legal standards.
