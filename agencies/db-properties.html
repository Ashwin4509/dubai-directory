About D&B Properties

Founded in Dubai in 2015, D&B Properties has grown into a mid-to-large brokerage covering most of the city's active property segments. The agency's 4.9/5 Google rating from over 3,500 reviews places it among the best-rated Dubai agencies by volume of verified client feedback — a meaningful indicator given that rating can be padded more easily with smaller review counts.

D&B covers the full market breadth — from residential apartments and family villas at mid-market price points, through to commercial office and retail space, and off-plan launches from developers active in the current cycle. This breadth makes the agency a practical option for clients who are still deciding between asset classes or who have mixed portfolios spanning both residential and commercial holdings.

The agency operates from Dubai and focuses its activity on established residential communities alongside the city's main commercial corridors. Their online presence on Bayut and Property Finder is active, and the website (dandbdubai.com) lists current inventory across all three segments. For a brokerage operating only since 2015, the review volume and rating consistency suggest a well-structured client service operation.
