About TEKCE Real Estate

TEKCE Real Estate is an international property agency with a strong presence across the UAE. The Dubai office is located in Düsseldorf Business Point, Al Barsha 1 — a central location with easy access to major residential communities and business hubs across the city.

The agency operates with over 250 agents who together serve clients across more than 30 languages. This multilingual capability is one of TEKCE's defining characteristics: it makes them especially accessible to buyers from Europe, Central Asia, the Middle East and beyond who want to research and transact Dubai property in their native language, without the friction of working through an interpreter or relying solely on English.

TEKCE holds RERA card number 63594, confirming registration with the Dubai Land Department as a licensed real estate brokerage. The agency covers residential property sales, off-plan projects, and investment property across the UAE, and can be verified independently through the DLD's official brokers list.

International buyers researching Dubai from overseas can also read TEKCE's customer reviews on Trustpilot, which provides independently collected feedback from clients across different markets.
