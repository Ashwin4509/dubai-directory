About Asyan Properties

Asyan Properties is a Dubai brokerage focusing on off-plan sales and international buyer advisory. The agency covers Dubai's key growth corridors — Dubai Hills Estate, Business Bay, Downtown Dubai and Jumeirah Village Circle — markets that collectively attract the majority of investor demand for new launch property in the city.

The agency's international buyer focus reflects the demographic reality of the Dubai off-plan market, where a large proportion of purchasers are based outside the UAE. Asyan Properties provides remote advisory and can guide non-resident buyers through the full purchase process, from initial project comparison through to DLD registration and post-handover management referrals.

As a boutique agency with a still-growing public review base, Asyan Properties is best assessed through direct consultation and specific enquiry. Frequently asked questions

What type of property does Asyan Properties specialise in?

Asyan Properties focuses on off-plan property and international buyer advisory in Dubai. Their focus areas include Dubai Hills Estate, Business Bay, Downtown Dubai and Jumeirah Village Circle — markets with active developer launch pipelines and strong investor demand from buyers both inside and outside the UAE.

Can Asyan Properties help buyers purchasing from outside the UAE?

Yes. Asyan Properties works with international buyers and can guide them through the Dubai purchase process remotely — including property shortlisting, payment plan comparison, DLD registration steps and post-handover management setup. Non-UAE residents can purchase freehold property in Dubai's designated freehold zones with no requirement to hold a UAE residency visa.

Is Dubai Hills Estate a good area for off-plan investment?

Dubai Hills Estate is a large-scale Emaar master-planned community built around an 18-hole golf course, with a mall, schools and established infrastructure. It has attracted strong price appreciation since its early off-plan phases and remains one of Dubai's most in-demand growth communities. Off-plan units in Dubai Hills tend to be priced at a premium to comparable areas like JVC, reflecting the master developer quality and lifestyle positioning. Asyan Properties covers Dubai Hills and can advise on current pricing and launch activity.
