About fäm Properties

Founded in 2009, fäm Properties has grown into one of the highest-volume brokerages in Dubai, with an agent roster and transaction count that places it among the market leaders by deal numbers. The agency's 15,000+ Google reviews — far more than most competitors — reflect the scale of client interactions the business handles annually.

The firm's most distinctive feature is DXBinteract, a property market data platform built on Dubai Land Department transaction records. Rather than relying on asking prices, DXBinteract shows verified sales data: what properties have actually sold for, how many transactions have occurred in a given community, and how prices have trended. This transparency helps buyers and sellers negotiate on evidence rather than estimates, and positions fäm Properties as a data-led agency in a market where anecdotal pricing has historically been common.

fäm Properties operates across all major Dubai market segments — secondary residential, off-plan launches, luxury properties and property management — making it suitable for a broad range of clients from first-time buyers to institutional investors. Their agent network covers most Dubai communities, and their developer relationships give them early access to off-plan inventory from major names including Emaar, Damac and Nakheel.

Frequently asked questions

What is DXBinteract and how does it help property buyers?
DXBinteract is a property data platform developed by fäm Properties that pulls verified transaction data from the Dubai Land Department. Instead of relying on asking prices — which can be inflated — buyers can see what properties in a given building or community have actually sold for, the volume of recent transactions and price trends over time. This makes it significantly easier to assess whether a listed property is priced fairly relative to the market.
Is fäm Properties a reliable agency in Dubai?
With over 15,000 Google reviews at 4.9/5, fäm Properties has one of the most extensive public rating profiles of any Dubai agency. That volume of consistent reviews across thousands of transactions suggests a reliable, systematised operation. As with any large brokerage, individual agent quality will vary — checking specific agent reviews before requesting a viewing is recommended.
Does fäm Properties handle off-plan property sales?
Yes. fäm Properties is active across both secondary and off-plan markets and has established relationships with major Dubai developers including Emaar, Damac and Nakheel. Their scale gives them access to a wide range of launches, and their DXBinteract data can help buyers benchmark off-plan prices against completed comparable properties.
How does fäm Properties compare to smaller boutique agencies in Dubai?
fäm Properties' advantage is scale and data: a large inventory, developer access and the DXBinteract platform for market intelligence. A boutique agency's advantage is typically deeper personal attention, specialist knowledge of a particular community or asset class, and greater flexibility. If data transparency and a wide selection are priorities, fäm Properties makes sense. If hyper-local expertise or dedicated service is paramount, a specialist boutique may be more appropriate.
