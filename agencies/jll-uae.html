About JLL UAE

Jones Lang LaSalle was founded in 1783 in London and is now a Fortune 500 company with operations in more than 80 countries. The UAE office is one of JLL's key Middle East hubs, covering both Dubai and Abu Dhabi with a broad range of commercial real estate services.

JLL UAE is widely recognised for its market research publications — quarterly reports on Dubai office, residential, retail and hospitality sectors that are frequently cited by investors, developers, government bodies and the media. This research capability gives JLL credibility as an advisory firm beyond just transactional brokerage.

The firm's transactional work spans office leasing (both tenant representation and landlord advisory), investment sales of commercial assets, retail leasing and industrial/logistics property. JLL UAE also manages significant property portfolios for institutional clients and provides RICS-compliant valuation services across all asset classes.

Like CBRE, JLL's primary UAE client base is institutional and corporate rather than private individual buyers. Their strength is in large, complex commercial transactions and long-term strategic advisory relationships. Private investors working at the high end of the commercial or residential market can engage JLL for advisory services.

Frequently asked questions

What does JLL do in the UAE?
JLL UAE covers commercial real estate advisory across office leasing, retail, industrial and logistics, investment sales, property management, valuations and market research. JLL is particularly well-regarded for its quarterly market research publications on the Dubai and Abu Dhabi commercial property markets.
Is JLL RERA registered in Dubai?
Yes. JLL UAE operates with RERA-registered agents for Dubai real estate transactions. You can verify current registration on the Dubai Land Department's website via the link above.
Does JLL work with private investors in Dubai?
JLL's primary focus in the UAE is institutional-grade real estate: large corporates, institutional investors, sovereign wealth funds and government-linked entities. Private investors at the high-value end can engage JLL for investment advisory, valuation and asset management services, particularly for commercial property. For residential sales and leasing, residential-specialist agencies in this directory will typically offer more appropriate hands-on service.
