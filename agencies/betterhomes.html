About Betterhomes

Founded in 1986, Betterhomes is one of the few Dubai real estate agencies that predates the city's modern property boom. Where many agencies launched after 2002 — when freehold ownership was opened to foreigners — Betterhomes has experience spanning multiple complete market cycles, including the 2008 correction and the recovery that followed.

The agency operates across both the sales and rental markets, with a large team of agents covering communities from Downtown Dubai and Dubai Marina to suburban family areas like Arabian Ranches and The Springs. This breadth makes Betterhomes a practical first contact for expatriates relocating to Dubai who need help navigating both the purchase and long-term rental options before deciding.

Betterhomes is also notable for its property management division, which handles end-to-end landlord services: tenant sourcing, lease execution, rent collection, maintenance and DTCM-compliant short-term rental management. For investors based outside the UAE, this full-management model is a key reason the agency maintains a large repeat-client base.

Frequently asked questions

Is Betterhomes a reputable agency in Dubai?
Betterhomes has been operating since 1986 — one of the longest track records of any Dubai agency. Its longevity across multiple market cycles, nearly a thousand Google reviews at 4.2/5, and a full-service offer covering sales, leasing, management and holiday homes indicate a well-established operation. As with any agency, individual agent quality varies, so checking specific agent reviews before committing is advisable.
What areas of Dubai does Betterhomes cover?
Betterhomes covers Dubai city-wide and also operates in Abu Dhabi. In Dubai, they are particularly active in JBR, Dubai Marina, Downtown, Business Bay, JVC, Arabian Ranches, The Springs, The Meadows and Dubai Hills Estate, making them a strong option for both waterfront and suburban family properties.
Does Betterhomes manage properties for overseas landlords?
Yes. Property management is a significant part of Betterhomes' business, designed specifically for landlords who are not based in the UAE. They handle tenant screening, lease execution, rent collection, maintenance and, for investors who want short-term rental income, DTCM-licensed holiday home management.
Can Betterhomes help me find a rental property in Dubai?
Yes. Betterhomes has one of the larger leasing teams in Dubai. Their website (bhomes.com) lists active rental properties, and you can contact agents directly. For popular communities in peak season, listings move quickly — reaching out to an agent rather than waiting for a listing to appear online is often faster.
How does Betterhomes compare to newer agencies like AX Capital or fäm Properties?
Betterhomes competes on depth of experience and breadth of services across the full market. Newer agencies like AX Capital and fäm tend to invest more heavily in digital marketing, data platforms and luxury branding. The right choice depends on your priority: if you want an agency with long-term market experience and end-to-end services including property management, Betterhomes is a strong candidate. If you prioritise tech-driven market data or a newer luxury brand, other agencies may suit better.
