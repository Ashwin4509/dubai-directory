About Hamptons International

Hamptons International brings over 150 years of estate agency experience from the UK to the Dubai market. The brand is particularly well-known among British expats relocating to Dubai, who arrive with familiarity and trust in the Hamptons name from home. This recognition shortcut matters in a market where buyers are often making significant financial decisions in an unfamiliar regulatory environment.

In Dubai, Hamptons' strength lies in residential sales and long-term lettings, particularly in communities that attract professional families: Arabian Ranches, The Springs, Jumeirah, Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah. The agency is less visible in the off-plan investment segment that dominates newer Dubai brokerages, reflecting a preference for established secondary-market properties and quality of service over transaction volume.

For buyers or tenants who value a structured, professional process with clear communication — and who may be making their first Dubai transaction without prior local experience — Hamptons offers the familiarity and reliability of a long-established global brand in a market that can otherwise feel opaque.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hamptons International good for UK expats buying in Dubai?
Hamptons is a natural fit for UK expats: the brand is familiar, agents understand the expectations and communication style of British buyers, and the agency's strength in family communities like Arabian Ranches, The Springs and Jumeirah aligns with what most UK families relocating to Dubai are looking for. They also have UK-to-Dubai relocation experience, which is useful for buyers making the move without visiting first.
Does Hamptons International handle rentals as well as sales?
Yes. Hamptons has historically been strong in residential lettings as well as sales — a reflection of their UK roots, where they operate across both markets. In Dubai they handle long-term residential lettings, property management and sales, making them a good single point of contact for expats who want to rent initially and buy later.
How does Hamptons compare to Allsopp & Allsopp in Dubai?
Both Hamptons and Allsopp & Allsopp are British-heritage agencies with strong reputations in Dubai's family and residential market. Allsopp has built a larger Dubai-specific profile and is more visible on social media. Hamptons carries the longer-established global brand. Both are solid choices for family residential property — it's worth speaking to both and choosing based on which team you connect with.
