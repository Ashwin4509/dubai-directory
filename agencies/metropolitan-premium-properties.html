About Metropolitan Premium Properties

Founded in 2008, Metropolitan Premium Properties structured itself with dedicated specialist divisions from early on — separate teams for off-plan launches, secondary market transactions and luxury property — rather than a single generalist agent pool. This division model allows clients to be matched with agents who have deep experience in their specific market segment, and improves consistency of advice across different transaction types.

Multilingual capability is a defining characteristic. Metropolitan operates teams fluent in Russian, Arabic, Chinese and several European languages, which is directly relevant in a Dubai market where a significant share of buyers arrive from Russia, China, India and Western Europe. Being able to explain complex legal and financial processes in a buyer's native language reduces misunderstanding and accelerates decision-making.

The agency's 2008 founding means they have operated through the 2008 correction, the 2014 slowdown and the post-2020 boom — experience that informs how they advise clients on risk, payment plans and market cycles. For international investors making their first or second Dubai purchase, that cycle experience can be more useful than pure transaction volume.

Frequently asked questions

Does Metropolitan Premium Properties have Russian-speaking agents?
Yes. Metropolitan Premium Properties operates Russian-speaking and CIS-market teams, making them one of the more commonly recommended agencies for Russian, Ukrainian and CIS-region buyers. This multilingual capability extends to Arabic, Chinese and several European languages — an advantage in a Dubai market where international buyers represent a large share of transactions.
What makes Metropolitan Premium Properties different from other Dubai agencies?
The dedicated division structure (separate off-plan, secondary and luxury teams) combined with multilingual capability are the primary differentiators. Rather than matching any buyer with any agent, the division model routes clients to specialists in their specific transaction type, which should produce more informed advice. For non-English-speaking international buyers in particular, the language capability reduces friction throughout the purchase process.
Is Metropolitan Premium Properties good for off-plan property?
Yes. Their dedicated off-plan division with multilingual teams makes them particularly useful for international investors who are evaluating multiple developer launches and need consistent guidance across all of them in their own language. Their 2008-founded experience across market cycles adds context to the advice.
