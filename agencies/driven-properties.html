About Driven Properties

Established in 2012 and headquartered in Downtown Dubai, Driven Properties built its positioning around investment-grade real estate — helping buyers identify, acquire and monetise property in Dubai with a focus on capital growth and rental yield rather than purely lifestyle purchasing. This investment lens differentiates the agency from residential-first brokerages and attracts a client base of portfolio builders alongside owner-occupiers.

Off-plan property is a core part of the Driven Properties offer. The agency maintains developer relationships across the market and provides clients with structured analysis of off-plan projects — supply pipeline, developer track record, projected yields and comparable completed sales — rather than simply presenting the developer's marketing materials. For international buyers who cannot easily visit every showroom, this advisory layer is practically useful.

The agency also covers commercial property and has operated international offices to service clients purchasing Dubai property from outside the UAE. Their multilingual team and experience with buyers from Europe, Asia and the Americas makes them a natural choice for non-resident investors navigating the Dubai purchase process for the first time.

Frequently asked questions

Is Driven Properties good for off-plan investment in Dubai?
Driven Properties has positioned itself around investment-grade property and maintains developer relationships across the off-plan market. Their advisory approach — assessing yield potential and developer track record rather than purely selling units — makes them more useful for buyers who want guidance beyond the developer's own marketing. With over 1,400 Google reviews at 4.5/5, they have a solid public track record.
Does Driven Properties work with overseas investors?
Yes. International buyers are a significant part of their client base and the agency has historically operated offices outside the UAE for that reason. They provide end-to-end support for non-resident buyers: area advisory, developer selection, legal process guidance and post-purchase management recommendations.
Does Driven Properties cover commercial property?
Yes. Commercial property is part of their offer alongside residential and off-plan. Their Downtown Dubai headquarters gives them natural exposure to the commercial leasing and sales market in that corridor and Business Bay.
