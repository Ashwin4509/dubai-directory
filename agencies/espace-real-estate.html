About Espace Real Estate

Founded in 2009, Espace Real Estate has spent 17+ years building specialist knowledge in Dubai's villa and townhouse communities. Rather than positioning as an all-Dubai agency, Espace concentrated its expertise in the family-oriented suburban areas that became popular with long-term residents and families: The Springs, The Meadows, Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills Estate. In those communities, their agents have typically transacted hundreds of times — a depth of community-specific knowledge that generalist agencies rarely match.

The practical value of community specialists is pricing precision. In a compound like Arabian Ranches, values differ significantly between phases, between unit types and even between specific street positions. An agent who has sold dozens of units in a phase can tell a buyer or seller exactly where their property sits in the micro-market, producing more accurate valuations and faster transactions than an agent relying on general area data.

Espace also covers the luxury segment within their villa community focus, including premium detached properties in Dubai Hills and larger Arabian Ranches homes. Their secondary-market emphasis suits buyers who want to see and inspect a finished property rather than buying off-plan.

Frequently asked questions

What communities does Espace Real Estate specialise in?
Espace specialises in The Springs, The Meadows, Emirates Living, Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills Estate — Dubai's established family villa communities. These are suburban townhouse and villa compounds popular with long-term residents and families. Their agent teams have deep transactional history in these specific communities, providing community-level pricing precision that generalist agencies rarely match.
Is Espace good for buying a family villa in Dubai?
For buyers targeting established villa communities, Espace is one of the stronger specialist options. Their agents typically know the micro-pricing within each phase of a community, understand which unit types hold value better and can provide genuine comparable sales evidence. For buyers who value deep community expertise over broad inventory, Espace is worth shortlisting alongside community specialist teams at Allsopp & Allsopp and haus & haus.
Does Espace deal in off-plan or only secondary market?
Espace is primarily a secondary-market agency — their strength is in completed villa communities where properties can be viewed and compared before purchase. They are not known as an off-plan launch specialist. For buyers who want to see a property before committing, Espace's secondary-market focus is an advantage over off-plan-heavy agencies.
