About Knight Frank UAE

Founded in London in 1896, Knight Frank has grown into a global partnership spanning more than 50 countries. The UAE practice is one of the firm's most active hubs in the Middle East, serving clients who require expertise across both residential and commercial real estate markets in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

The Dubai team is well known for its annual research publications, including the Prime Global Cities Index and the Wealth Report — widely cited by investors, developers, and media as a benchmark for luxury real estate trends in the region. This research-led approach differentiates Knight Frank from transaction-only brokerages and makes it a common first call for international buyers entering the Dubai market.

Knight Frank UAE handles mandates across the full property lifecycle: from helping an international investor identify a prime villa on Palm Jumeirah, to advising a multinational corporation on its DIFC office lease, to providing an independent valuation for a bank financing a large residential development.

Frequently asked questions

How does Knight Frank compare to Savills in Dubai?
Both are global consultancies with established Dubai operations. Knight Frank has historically had a stronger prime residential and cross-border investor presence in the UAE, and its annual Wealth Report carries significant weight in the luxury market. Savills tends to be stronger in the UK, and its Dubai commercial and corporate occupier practice is well regarded. For a prime villa purchase or an international investment acquisition, Knight Frank's network may be an advantage. For mid-market residential or a complex commercial lease, comparing both on their specific track record in that asset class is advisable.
Is Knight Frank UAE registered with RERA?
Knight Frank UAE operates within Dubai's regulatory framework. Always verify current RERA registration directly with the Dubai Land Department at dubailand.gov.ae before entering any transaction — registration status can change and the DLD portal holds the live record.
Is it safe to use an international consultancy like Knight Frank over a local boutique in Dubai?
Both have legitimate roles in the market. A global firm like Knight Frank brings brand credibility, a wide international buyer network, and in-house research that smaller agencies cannot match — useful if you are selling a prime property and need international exposure, or if you are a foreign investor who wants a recognisable counterparty. A local boutique often has deeper neighbourhood knowledge, faster response times and more flexibility on fees. The decision typically comes down to asset class, price point and whether you need cross-border reach.
What is Knight Frank's Wealth Report and why does it matter for Dubai buyers?
The Knight Frank Wealth Report is an annual publication tracking ultra-high-net-worth individual behaviour, prime property price indices and global wealth migration. It is widely cited by developers, investors and media as a leading indicator of luxury real estate demand. Dubai consistently ranks among the top cities globally in the report, and the data has become a standard reference point for buyers trying to benchmark Dubai prime prices against cities like London, New York and Singapore.
Does Knight Frank handle off-plan property in Dubai?
Yes. Knight Frank UAE's project marketing division works with developers to sell off-plan launches to their global network of investors. They also advise buyers on which off-plan projects to consider, though as with any brokerage, buyers should confirm whether the recommendation is fee-neutral or includes a developer commission.

Dubai has thousands of RERA-registered real estate brokerages — the DLD reports numbers in the range of several thousand active broker registrations at any given time, covering everything from large multinationals like Knight Frank to single-person boutiques.
