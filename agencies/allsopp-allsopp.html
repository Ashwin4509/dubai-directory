About Allsopp & Allsopp

Founded in 2008 by Lewis Allsopp, the agency was built around the principle that real estate in Dubai deserved the same structured, professional approach used in more mature markets like the UK. The ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification — achieved and maintained by Allsopp & Allsopp — is a practical expression of that philosophy, requiring documented processes, regular audits and measurable service standards.

The brokerage has built its reputation primarily in the secondary residential market — properties that already exist, that buyers can view and inspect before purchase. This contrasts with the heavy off-plan focus of many Dubai competitors, and appeals to buyers who want certainty before committing. The agency is particularly strong in suburban family communities: The Springs, The Meadows, Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills Estate are areas where their agent teams have deep local knowledge.

Allsopp & Allsopp also operates a short-term leasing and mortgage referral service, positioning itself as a broader housing partner rather than a pure transaction broker. Their agent count and transaction volume make them one of the mid-to-large tier Dubai agencies, visible across Bayut, Property Finder and their own website.

Frequently asked questions

Is Allsopp & Allsopp a good agency in Dubai?
Allsopp & Allsopp has a strong reputation for customer service and transparency. Their ISO 9001:2015 certification is unusual in the Dubai market and signals documented processes rather than ad hoc service. Over 1,600 Google reviews at 4.4/5 give them one of the stronger public rating profiles among established Dubai brokerages. Agent quality will vary, so requesting a specific agent by name based on reviews is worthwhile.
What does ISO certification mean for a real estate agency?
ISO 9001:2015 is an international quality management standard requiring a company to document its processes, set measurable targets and undergo independent third-party audits. For a real estate agency, this typically means structured processes around how listings are handled, how clients are communicated with, and how complaints are resolved. It is not common among Dubai real estate brokerages, where most operate without formal quality accreditation.
Does Allsopp & Allsopp focus on off-plan or secondary market property?
Allsopp & Allsopp is primarily a secondary-market agency — they focus on completed, existing properties rather than off-plan launches. This suits buyers who want to physically inspect a property before purchase, see the quality of the build and understand the community before committing. They do carry some off-plan listings but are not a specialist off-plan brokerage.
Which family communities in Dubai is Allsopp & Allsopp known for?
The Springs, The Meadows, Emirates Living and Arabian Ranches are the communities most associated with Allsopp & Allsopp's specialist knowledge. Dubai Hills Estate and JVC are also areas where the agency has a strong presence. These are predominantly villa and townhouse communities popular with families, which aligns with the agency's positioning.
