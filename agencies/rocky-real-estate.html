About Rocky Real Estate

Rocky Real Estate has operated in the Dubai market as a full-service brokerage, covering both residential and commercial property across the city's established communities. The agency offers the core services that buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants need: sales, leasing and ongoing property management for owners who prefer to have their properties managed professionally.

In Dubai's competitive brokerage market, Rocky Real Estate positions itself as an accessible, practical choice for clients across a range of budget points and property types. Their service covers Dubai's established residential communities alongside commercial office and retail space for business occupiers.

For investors, the combination of sales, leasing and management under one roof means that an investor can acquire a property through Rocky Real Estate and transition it to their management team without changing their service provider — a convenience that some buyers value, particularly for smaller portfolios.

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Rocky Real Estate cover in Dubai?
Rocky Real Estate covers residential and commercial property across a broad range of Dubai communities, including established areas such as Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, JVC and villa communities. They operate as a full-service brokerage with both sales and leasing capabilities city-wide.
Does Rocky Real Estate offer property management?
Yes. Rocky Real Estate offers property management services for landlords and investors alongside their sales and leasing business. This covers day-to-day management, tenant relations and maintenance coordination.
Is Rocky Real Estate RERA-registered?
Rocky Real Estate operates as a licensed brokerage in Dubai. Always verify their current RERA registration status directly through the Dubai Land Department's smart services portal before transacting.
