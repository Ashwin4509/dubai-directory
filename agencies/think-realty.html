About Think Realty

Think Realty is a Dubai residential brokerage covering apartments, townhouses and villas across several of Dubai's most active residential markets. The agency handles both the transactional and ongoing sides of residential property — sales, leasing and property management — which means clients don't need to engage a separate management firm after completing a purchase or letting a unit.

The agency's presence across Dubai Marina, JBR, JVC and Business Bay reflects the core apartment markets where demand from both owner-occupiers and buy-to-let investors has been consistently strong. These areas benefit from established infrastructure, public transport links and high tenant demand from professionals working in the DIFC and SZR corridors, which makes them reliable anchors for a residential-focused brokerage.

Think Realty's 4.8 Google rating from 181 reviews reflects strong client satisfaction relative to their transaction volume. For a residential brokerage of their size, this rating and review count indicate genuine quality across a meaningful number of client interactions rather than a small handful of hand-picked reviewers. Buyers, sellers and landlords looking for responsive, residential-specialist service in these areas will find Think Realty a practical option.

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Think Realty cover in Dubai?

Think Realty covers established residential markets including Dubai Marina, JBR, Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), Business Bay, Dubai Silicon Oasis and adjoining areas. They handle both sales and leasing and can advise on pricing and yield comparables across the areas they cover.

Does Think Realty offer property management?

Yes. Property management is a core part of Think Realty's offer. Their service covers tenant sourcing and vetting, lease preparation and EJARI registration, rent collection, maintenance coordination and end-of-tenancy processes. For landlords who live outside Dubai or who don't want to manage the day-to-day tenant relationship, this provides a single point of contact for all ongoing property administration.

What are rental yields like in JVC compared to Dubai Marina?

JVC (Jumeirah Village Circle) generally offers higher rental yields relative to purchase price — typically in the 6–8% range — because entry prices are lower and tenant demand from mid-income residents is strong. Dubai Marina is a premium address with higher capital values, which translates to lower yields in the 4–6% range, though demand is deep and secondary market liquidity is high. Think Realty covers both areas and can provide current comparables for specific unit types.
