About haus & haus

Founded in 2013, haus & haus built its reputation in the Dubai residential market by prioritising client relationships over transaction volume. The name reflects that positioning — an agency that focuses on the home, not just the deal. With 4.8/5 from over 2,600 Google reviews, they have a strong and consistent public rating that spans more than a decade of operation.

The agency covers the full residential spectrum from mid-market apartments to high-end villas and penthouses, with a team structure that includes dedicated specialist agents for specific communities. That community depth is a practical advantage for buyers or tenants who want an agent who genuinely knows the area — its micro-pricing, the quality differences between buildings, school catchments and the realities of day-to-day living there.

Property management is a meaningful part of the haus & haus offer. For investors who purchase Dubai property as a buy-to-let, the ability to hand off tenant management to the same agency that handled the sale is a convenience that reduces administrative friction. Their management service covers tenant vetting, lease execution, rent collection and maintenance — the main tasks that make remote landlord ownership practical.
