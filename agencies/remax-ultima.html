About RE/MAX Ultima

RE/MAX Ultima operates under the RE/MAX franchise, one of the world's largest real estate networks by agent count. The Dubai office is based at Al Salam Tower in Dubai Internet City, a modern commercial hub that houses many of the city's international businesses and consultancies.

The agency serves buyers, sellers and investors across Dubai's residential market, leveraging the RE/MAX global network to connect international buyers with Dubai property opportunities. This can be particularly useful for clients based outside the UAE who are researching or purchasing Dubai property remotely, as RE/MAX's worldwide presence means other RE/MAX offices in their home country can facilitate the relationship.

For buyers and sellers already in Dubai, RE/MAX Ultima provides standard brokerage services across residential sales and leasing, with access to the RE/MAX global listing systems and marketing platforms that help promote Dubai properties internationally.

Frequently asked questions

Is RE/MAX Ultima RERA registered in Dubai?
RE/MAX Ultima operates under the globally recognised RE/MAX franchise in Dubai. To verify their current RERA registration, check the Dubai Land Department's licensed brokers list at dubailand.gov.ae using their agency name or licence number.
What makes a RE/MAX franchise useful for international buyers?
RE/MAX is one of the world's largest real estate networks by agent count, operating across more than 110 countries. This means buyers from the US, Europe, Asia or elsewhere often have a RE/MAX office near them in their home country, which can provide local-language support and act as a bridge to the Dubai office before they visit. For overseas buyers researching Dubai property, this referral network can reduce friction in getting started.
Where is RE/MAX Ultima based in Dubai?
RE/MAX Ultima is based at Office 2608, Al Salam Tower in Dubai Internet City — a modern commercial area near Dubai Media City and Knowledge Village, with easy access to key residential and business communities including Dubai Marina, JLT and JVC.
