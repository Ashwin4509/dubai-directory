About Exclusive Links Real Estate Brokers

Founded in 2005, Exclusive Links Real Estate Brokers has built its business around the established villa communities that were launched during Dubai's first major residential expansion — Arabian Ranches, The Springs, The Meadows and Jumeirah Islands among them. The agency's longevity in these areas means its agents have often sold the same properties multiple times across different market cycles, giving buyers access to a depth of comparable transaction data that is difficult for newer brokerages to replicate.

Residential leasing is a significant part of the business alongside sales. Exclusive Links connects landlords with tenants in their core villa communities and manages the end-to-end leasing process including DLD registration, EJARI, lease renewals and rent increase disputes under the RDC framework. For owner-investors who want a passive income from a Dubai villa without dealing directly with tenants, the property management service provides a single point of contact.

The agency's 4.8 Google rating from 465 reviews reflects consistent client satisfaction over many transactions. Operating as an independent brokerage — rather than as part of a large franchise — means the agency has maintained a community-specialist approach rather than spreading across every district in Dubai. Buyers and landlords who want an agent with genuine local knowledge of a specific community rather than a generalist who covers everywhere will find this a practical advantage.

Frequently asked questions

Is Exclusive Links good for buying in Arabian Ranches?

Exclusive Links has covered Arabian Ranches since its earliest phases and agents there have sold across all sub-communities — from Palmera and Saheel to Alvorada and Casa. Their long presence means they can compare recent transaction prices across phases and advise on which sub-communities tend to achieve better resale growth, which is genuinely useful for buyers choosing between broadly similar options.

Does Exclusive Links manage rental properties?

Yes. Property management is one of Exclusive Links' core services. They handle tenant sourcing, lease renewals, rent collection, maintenance coordination and DLD registration. For landlords with a villa in a managed community like Arabian Ranches or Springs who don't want to handle day-to-day management themselves, this is a practical option that removes the landlord from the tenant relationship.

What areas does Exclusive Links cover?

Exclusive Links covers established Dubai communities including Arabian Ranches (all phases), The Springs, The Meadows, Jumeirah Islands, Jumeirah Village Circle and Jumeirah Golf Estates. Their depth is strongest in the villa communities of south-west Dubai where they have been active since those communities launched.

How long has Exclusive Links been operating?

Exclusive Links was established in 2005, making it one of the longer-standing independent brokerages in Dubai. The agency has operated through the 2008–2009 correction, the 2014–2015 downturn, the COVID period and the post-2021 price recovery — a range of market conditions that gives their agents a depth of context that newer brokerages have not yet accumulated.
