About Aeon & Trisl

Founded in 2013, Aeon & Trisl has grown into one of Dubai's higher-volume off-plan brokerages by building developer relationships that provide early allocation access to major launches. The agency's partnerships with Emaar, DAMAC, Nakheel and Sobha mean their agents can offer clients units at launch pricing before projects reach the broader market — which is a practical advantage for investors who want first-mover pricing on quality projects.

International buyers account for a significant share of Aeon & Trisl's client base. The agency operates with a multilingual team and has experience guiding buyers through the Dubai purchase process remotely — including power of attorney structures for clients who cannot attend the DLD registration in person, advice on mortgage eligibility for non-residents, and post-handover property management referrals. Their review volume of 955 on Google reflects a high transaction throughput which spans many years of consistent activity.

Beyond new launches, Aeon & Trisl also covers the secondary market, helping investors who purchased off-plan near completion to sell or rent their units. This secondary capability means clients don't need to seek a different agency once their property is ready — the same team that handled the original purchase can assist with exit or tenancy setup, which is useful for investors managing UAE property from outside the country.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aeon & Trisl good for off-plan investment in Dubai?

Aeon & Trisl is one of Dubai's higher-volume off-plan brokerages with longstanding developer relationships across Emaar, DAMAC, Nakheel and Sobha. This typically means early allocation access to popular launches before units reach the general public. For investors targeting off-plan, this allocation access can be a practical advantage over smaller brokerages that may only receive remaining stock after preferred allocations have been distributed.

What payment plan structures do Dubai developers offer?

Most Dubai off-plan developers offer a construction-linked plan where the buyer pays in instalments tied to milestones — typically 10–20% on booking, then tranches at foundation, structural and handover stages. Post-handover payment plans, where a portion of the price is paid after handover over one to five years, are also common and reduce the capital required upfront. Aeon & Trisl can advise on which current projects carry which plan structures.

Does Aeon & Trisl work with international buyers?

Yes. International buyers make up a significant proportion of Aeon & Trisl's client base. The agency has multilingual teams and experience with the Dubai purchase process for non-residents — including POA structures for buyers who cannot attend the signing in person, mortgage eligibility advice and post-handover management referrals.

What is the difference between JVC and Dubai Hills for off-plan investment?

JVC (Jumeirah Village Circle) has lower entry prices and strong rental demand from mid-income residents — it tends to deliver better rental yields relative to price paid. Dubai Hills Estate is a master-planned community with golf, retail and schools that has attracted stronger capital appreciation and a higher-income resident base. The right choice depends on whether the buyer prioritises yield or capital growth, and their budget — JVC entry prices are typically significantly lower.
