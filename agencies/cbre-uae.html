About CBRE UAE

CBRE Group was founded in 1906 and is now the world's largest commercial real estate services firm by revenue, employing over 130,000 people globally. The UAE operation is one of CBRE's Middle East hubs, with a primary Dubai office and a significant Abu Dhabi presence.

In the UAE market, CBRE is best known for its institutional-grade commercial real estate work: advising corporate occupiers on office leasing strategies, facilitating major investment sales of commercial assets, conducting property valuations for banks and institutional clients, and managing large commercial and mixed-use portfolios. The firm publishes widely-read market research reports on Dubai and Abu Dhabi, which are used as reference points by investors, developers and government entities.

CBRE UAE also has a residential advisory capability, though this is primarily oriented toward high-value portfolio transactions, corporate relocation advisory and prime residential investment rather than day-to-day sales and leasing. Buyers and investors working at the institutional or ultra-high-net-worth level will find CBRE's global network and research depth particularly valuable.

Frequently asked questions

What does CBRE do in the UAE?
CBRE's UAE operations cover commercial real estate across its full service line: office and retail leasing, industrial and logistics, investment sales, property and facilities management, valuations and appraisals, and residential advisory. Dubai is CBRE's primary UAE hub, with operations also in Abu Dhabi.
Is CBRE UAE RERA registered?
Yes. CBRE UAE operates with RERA-registered agents for all Dubai real estate transactions. As with all agencies, you can verify current RERA registration on the Dubai Land Department's website via the link above.
Does CBRE handle residential property in Dubai?
CBRE UAE has a residential advisory division alongside its dominant commercial operations. Their residential team covers high-value residential sales, investment advisory and prime market research. For day-to-day residential transactions, boutique residential agencies typically offer more hands-on service — CBRE is most often engaged for larger portfolio transactions, corporate relocations and institutional residential investment.
