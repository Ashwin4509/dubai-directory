About Luxhabitat Sotheby's International Realty

Luxhabitat Sotheby's International Realty operates at the top tier of the Dubai residential market, focusing almost exclusively on properties above AED 5 million. The Sotheby's International Realty affiliation is meaningful: it gives the agency access to a global referral network that channels ultra-high-net-worth buyers from financial hubs worldwide toward Dubai's most exclusive addresses.

The agency's core markets are Emirates Hills — Dubai's closest equivalent to Bel Air, with large private villa plots — Palm Jumeirah signature villas, Jumeirah Bay Island and the upper floors of Downtown Dubai's landmark towers. These are markets where brand recognition, discretion and international reach matter more than volume of transactions.

For sellers of trophy assets in Dubai, the Sotheby's network provides access to buyers who might not be browsing Property Finder — international art collectors, family offices and institutional wealth managers who purchase Dubai property alongside other global assets. For buyers at this level, Luxhabitat's off-market access is a genuine differentiator, since many significant transactions at the top end of the Dubai market never appear on public portals.

Frequently asked questions

What does Sotheby's International Realty mean for buyers in Dubai?
The Sotheby's International Realty affiliation means Luxhabitat properties are marketed through a global referral network reaching high-net-worth buyers in London, New York, Hong Kong and beyond. For sellers of trophy assets in Dubai, this international reach is a meaningful advantage. For buyers, it means the agency has access to off-market listings that may not appear on public portals.
Is Luxhabitat only for buyers with very large budgets?
Luxhabitat focuses primarily on the upper end of the Dubai market — properties typically above AED 5–10 million. They are most active in Emirates Hills, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Bay Island and Downtown Dubai penthouses. If your budget is below that range, agencies like Driven Properties, AX Capital or Allsopp & Allsopp are better starting points.
How does Luxhabitat Sotheby's compare to Knight Frank or Savills in Dubai?
All three operate in the top tier of the Dubai market. Knight Frank and Savills bring deep institutional research capabilities and corporate occupier services alongside residential. Luxhabitat Sotheby's is purely residential luxury, with the Sotheby's brand carrying particular weight for international collectors buying Dubai property as part of a global portfolio.
