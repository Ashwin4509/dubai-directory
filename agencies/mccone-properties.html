About McCone Properties

McCone Properties was established in 2014 and has built one of the strongest service reputations of any Dubai brokerage. A 4.9 Google rating from 1,121 reviews is unusual at this volume — most agencies see ratings erode as review counts climb past 500, making it progressively harder to offset a single below-average experience. McCone's consistency at this level indicates a systematically strong client experience embedded in how the agency operates, not an occasional lucky run of good feedback.

The agency covers the premium residential markets along Dubai's western corridor — Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah and Downtown Dubai — as well as Dubai Hills Estate and its surrounding communities. These are among Dubai's most liquid residential markets with deep buyer and tenant demand, which gives McCone's clients the confidence that the properties they purchase through the agency can be resold or leased relatively quickly. The agency handles both sales and luxury leasing, and can assist with the end-to-end transaction from initial property shortlisting through to DLD registration.

McCone Properties is also notable for the quality of its agents relative to its size. Dubai's brokerage market has historically suffered from high agent turnover and inconsistent training, which means client experience varies significantly at larger agencies. McCone's review scores suggest they have maintained a more consistent agent standard — a signal buyers should factor in when choosing who to work with for a significant transaction.

Frequently asked questions

Why does McCone Properties have such a high rating?

McCone Properties has a 4.9 Google rating from over 1,100 reviews — a combination of high score and high volume that is rare in Dubai's brokerage market. Most agencies see ratings drop as review volume rises. McCone's consistency at 4.9 across this volume indicates a systematically strong client experience rather than a few exceptional reviews inflating an otherwise average score. The agency is known for responsive communication and agents who follow through on commitments.

What areas does McCone Properties specialise in?

McCone Properties has deep knowledge across Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai and Dubai Hills Estate. These are among Dubai's most established and liquid residential markets, with strong secondary sale volumes and consistent rental demand. The agency also covers newer growth areas in response to client interest.

Is Dubai Marina a good area to buy an apartment?

Dubai Marina is one of Dubai's most established waterfront residential communities with deep secondary market liquidity, strong rental demand from professionals and good infrastructure. It is not the cheapest market, but it is among the most reliable in terms of being able to resell quickly. For buyers who value liquidity and lifestyle alongside investment return, Marina is consistently cited as a top-tier choice. McCone Properties can advise on which buildings within Marina offer the best combination of price and tenant demand.

Does McCone Properties handle off-plan purchases?

While McCone is primarily known as a residential brokerage focused on the ready market, they can advise clients on off-plan options and make developer introductions. Their strength is helping buyers evaluate the comparison between a ready property now versus an off-plan alternative — factoring in payment timing, construction risk, rental yield foregone during construction and developer track record.
