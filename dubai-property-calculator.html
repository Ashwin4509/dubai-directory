Key numbers to know

DLD Transfer Fee

4% of the purchase price, paid by the buyer at the trustee office on transfer day. The largest single cost of buying in Dubai.

Agency Commission

2% of the purchase price, standard across Dubai. Paid by the buyer to the buyer's agent at or before transfer.

Min. Deposit (Mortgage)

25% for non-UAE nationals on properties up to AED 5M. 35% for properties above AED 5M. Cash buyers have no minimum.

Good Rental Yield

Gross yield of 6–8% is considered strong in Dubai. JVC, Business Bay and Sports City typically offer the highest yields in the mid-market segment.

Service Charges

Annual fee for building maintenance, paid by the owner. Ranges from AED 8/sqft (budget buildings) to AED 40+/sqft (premium towers). Check before buying — it directly affects net yield.

Golden Visa Threshold

Property worth AED 2M+ (equity, not purchase price if mortgaged) qualifies for a 10-year UAE Golden Visa. AED 750K+ qualifies for a 2-year investor visa.
